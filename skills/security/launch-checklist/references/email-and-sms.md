# Stage 10: Email and Text Messages

From the launch readiness guide at https://ducktate.com/learn/launch-readiness/ (checked 2026-10-09). Not legal advice. Each item is tagged [Must do], [Should do] or [If it applies] and lists its sources.

**In a Lovable project:** Transactional emails sent from the project (auth emails, receipts, notifications) must stay free of promotions. Check sender domain authentication when a custom email domain is set up.

## Checklist

- [ ] Sort every email your app sends into transactional or commercial, and keep promotions out of receipts, password resets and account notices. [Must do] Sources: 16 CFR 316.3 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/316.3), FTC CAN-SPAM guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)
- [ ] Put a valid postal address in the footer of every marketing email: a street address, a registered PO box or a registered private mailbox. [Must do] Sources: FTC CAN-SPAM guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), 15 U.S.C. 7704 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/7704)
- [ ] Put a clear unsubscribe link in every marketing email, and keep it working for at least 30 days after you send. [Must do] Sources: FTC CAN-SPAM guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), 15 U.S.C. 7704 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/7704)
- [ ] Store every opt-out in your own database, and make every send script check that list before it sends. [Must do] Sources: FTC CAN-SPAM guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)
- [ ] Stop marketing email within 10 business days of an opt-out, and aim for the same day, because Yahoo asks for 2 days. [Must do] Sources: FTC CAN-SPAM guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), Yahoo sender best practices (https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/)
- [ ] Use a true From name and reply address, a subject line that matches the content, and a clear label when the email is an ad. [Must do] Sources: FTC CAN-SPAM guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)
- [ ] Set up SPF and DKIM for your sending domain (Gmail requires at least one from every sender), and add DMARC even if you send fewer than 5,000 emails a day. [Should do] Sources: Google sender guidelines (https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126), Yahoo sender best practices (https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/)
- [ ] If you send more than 5,000 emails a day to Gmail or Outlook.com, publish a DMARC record aligned with your From domain, on top of SPF and DKIM. [Must do] Sources: Google sender guidelines (https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126), Microsoft postmaster policies (https://substrate.office.com/ip-domain-management-snds/postmaster/Policies)
- [ ] Add the List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post one-click headers to marketing email, plus a visible unsubscribe link in the body. [Should do] Sources: Google sender guidelines (https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126), Yahoo sender best practices (https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/)
- [ ] Keep your spam complaint rate below 0.1%, and never let it reach 0.3%. [Should do] Sources: Google sender guidelines (https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126), Yahoo sender best practices (https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/)
- [ ] Review what your email vendor and any agency send in your name, because you stay responsible for it. [Should do] Sources: FTC CAN-SPAM guide (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)
- [ ] Get prior consent before you send marketing email to people in the EU or UK, and use the soft opt-in only for your own similar products sent to existing customers. [If it applies] Sources: ePrivacy Art. 13 (UK-hosted copy) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/58/article/13/data.html), PECR reg. 22 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/22)
- [ ] For recipients in Canada, have express or implied consent, name yourself and your contact details, and honor unsubscribes within 10 business days. [If it applies] Sources: CASL s. 6 (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/page-1.html), CASL s. 11 (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/page-2.html)
- [ ] Before you send any marketing text, collect written consent that names the phone number and says consent is not a condition of buying. [If it applies] Sources: 47 CFR 64.1200 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/64.1200), 47 U.S.C. 227 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/227)
- [ ] Honor STOP, unsubscribe and similar replies to texts at once, even though the rule allows up to 10 business days. [If it applies] Sources: 47 CFR 64.1200 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/64.1200), FCC draft order (https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-424844A1.pdf)
