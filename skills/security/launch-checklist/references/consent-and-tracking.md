# Stage 4: Consent Before Tracking

From the launch readiness guide at https://ducktate.com/learn/launch-readiness/ (checked 2026-10-09). Not legal advice. Each item is tagged [Must do], [Should do] or [If it applies] and lists its sources.

**In a Lovable project:** Check the root layout and route heads for analytics, pixels and chat widgets, and verify in the published app with Playwright: load the page, reject consent, reload, and inspect network requests and cookies.

## Checklist

- [ ] Keep a one-page list of every tracker on your site and app: what it is for, what data it sends, whether it waits for consent, and whether you have a contract with the vendor. [Must do] Sources: CPPA on Tractor Supply (https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250930.html), CPPA on Honda (https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250312.html)
- [ ] For visitors in the EU, load no analytics, replay, ad pixel or chat widget that stores or reads data on their device until they accept. [If it applies] Sources: EDPB Guidelines 2/2023 (https://edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2024-10/edpb_guidelines_202302_technical_scope_art_53_eprivacydirective_v2_en_0.pdf), PostHog data collection (https://posthog.com/docs/privacy/data-collection)
- [ ] Put a Reject button on the same layer as Accept, with the same size and style. [If it applies] Sources: EDPB cookie banner report (https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2023-01/edpb_20230118_report_cookie_banner_taskforce_en.pdf), CNIL on Google, 2025 (https://www.cnil.fr/en/cookies-and-advertisements-inserted-between-emails-google-fined-325-million-euros-cnil)
- [ ] If you have a banner or an opt-out, test that Reject really stops tracking: click Reject, reload, and check the Network and Cookies tabs in your browser's developer tools. [If it applies] Sources: California AG on Healthline (https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-announces-largest-ccpa-settlement-date-secures-155)
- [ ] Turn session replay off in your code, not only in the vendor dashboard, until you have a reason and a consent step. [Should do] Sources: PostHog config (https://posthog.com/docs/libraries/js/config), PostHog replay controls (https://posthog.com/docs/session-replay/how-to-control-which-sessions-you-record)
- [ ] If you record sessions, show notice and get consent before recording starts. [If it applies] Sources: DLA Piper on Javier (https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2022/06/javier-v-assurance-iq-llc-et-al)
- [ ] If you record sessions, mask all inputs and sensitive text, block payment, login, settings and chat pages, and scrub network request bodies. [If it applies] Sources: PostHog replay privacy (https://posthog.com/docs/session-replay/privacy), Clarity masking (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/clarity/setup-and-installation/clarity-masking)
- [ ] Turn off autocapture and send only the named events you chose. [Should do] Sources: PostHog data collection (https://posthog.com/docs/privacy/data-collection)
- [ ] If the CCPA covers you and you sell or share data, honor Global Privacy Control as an opt-out and apply it to the whole logged-in account. [If it applies] Sources: 11 CCR 7025 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/11-CCR-7025), California AG on Sephora (https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-announces-settlement-sephora-part-ongoing-enforcement), California AG on Disney (https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/california-wont-let-it-go-attorney-general-bonta-announces-275-million)
- [ ] Do not ask for ID just to opt out, and test your opt-out form after every deploy. [If it applies] Sources: CPPA on Todd Snyder (https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250506.html), CPPA on Honda (https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250312.html)
- [ ] If you use Microsoft Clarity and have visitors from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, send the consent signal through its Consent API or a supported consent tool. [If it applies] Sources: Clarity consent mode (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/clarity/setup-and-installation/consent-mode)
- [ ] Do not treat SB 690 as permission for session replay or chat recording; get consent before any tool captures what users type. [Should do] Sources: Baker Donelson on SB 690 (https://www.bakerdonelson.com/sb-690-signed-into-law-what-it-means-for-cipa/web-tracking-litigation), WilmerHale on SB 690 (https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/blogs/wilmerhale-privacy-and-cybersecurity-law/20261006-cipa-reform-signed-into-law)
