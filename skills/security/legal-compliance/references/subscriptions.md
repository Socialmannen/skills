# Stage 9: Subscriptions and Cancellation

From the launch readiness guide at https://ducktate.com/learn/launch-readiness/ (checked 2026-10-09). Not legal advice. Each item is tagged [Must do], [Should do] or [If it applies] and lists its sources.

**In a Lovable project:** Lovable's Stripe and Paddle integrations provide checkout and customer portals; check that the screens before checkout and the cancel path in the published app meet these items, and test a full sign-up and cancel as a test user.

## Checklist

- [ ] Show the price, how often it is charged, that it renews automatically, and how to cancel right next to the Subscribe button, before the card form. [If it applies] Sources: 15 U.S.C. 8403 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/8403), Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602)
- [ ] Get consent with a button or an unticked checkbox that names the charge, not with a line hidden in your terms. [If it applies] Sources: 15 U.S.C. 8403 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/8403), Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602)
- [ ] Save proof of each consent (time, user, price shown, terms version) for at least 3 years, or 1 year after the subscription ends if that is longer. [Should do] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602)
- [ ] Send a confirmation email right after sign-up with the price, the renewal terms and how to cancel. [Should do] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602), Minn. Stat. 325G.57 (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/325G.57)
- [ ] Let anyone who signed up online cancel online, at once, from a visible link or button, with no phone call, chat or wait. [If it applies] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602), 15 U.S.C. 8403 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/8403)
- [ ] If you show a save offer, keep a cancel button on the same screen as the offer. [Should do] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602), Stripe customer portal (https://docs.stripe.com/customer-management)
- [ ] For a free trial or promo price longer than 30 days, email the customer before it ends: 3 to 21 days before for California, 5 to 30 days before for Minnesota. [If it applies] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602), Minn. Stat. 325G.57 (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/325G.57)
- [ ] For a plan with a first term of a year or more, send a renewal notice 15 to 45 days before it renews. [If it applies] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602)
- [ ] Send every subscriber a yearly reminder with the product, the price, how often it is charged and how to cancel. [Should do] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602), Minn. Stat. 325G.57 (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/325G.57)
- [ ] Give subscribers 7 to 30 days notice before a price change, with how to cancel. [Should do] Sources: Cal. 17602 (mirror) (https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_17602), Skadden on the NYC rule (https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/09/new-york-citys-click-to-cancel-rule)
- [ ] If you sell subscriptions inside a mobile app, check the screen before purchase against Apple guideline 3.1.2 and Google Play's subscription policy. [If it applies] Sources: Apple guidelines (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), Google Play subscriptions (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533)
- [ ] Before each release, sign up and cancel as a test user, and confirm that no further charge is scheduled. [Should do] Sources: Stripe customer portal (https://docs.stripe.com/customer-management), 15 U.S.C. 8403 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/8403)
- [ ] Do not treat the vacated click-to-cancel rule as permission; follow ROSCA and the law of the states where most of your users live. [Should do] Sources: Eighth Circuit opinion (https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/25/07/243137P.pdf), FTC on Amazon (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-secures-historic-25-billion-settlement-against-amazon), Skadden (https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/09/new-york-citys-click-to-cancel-rule)
