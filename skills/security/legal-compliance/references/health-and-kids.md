# Stage 5: Sensitive Data: Health and Children

From the launch readiness guide at https://ducktate.com/learn/launch-readiness/ (checked 2026-10-09). Not legal advice. Each item is tagged [Must do], [Should do] or [If it applies] and lists its sources.

## Checklist

- [ ] Write down whether your app collects any health information, including symptoms, medications, fertility, mood or mental health, and keep the answer with your other launch notes. [Must do] Sources: FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule), FTC on GoodRx (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/ftc-enforcement-action-bar-goodrx-sharing-consumers-sensitive-health-info-advertising)
- [ ] Remove ad pixels and ad SDKs from every screen and page where users enter or read health information. [If it applies] Sources: FTC on GoodRx (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/ftc-enforcement-action-bar-goodrx-sharing-consumers-sensitive-health-info-advertising), FTC on BetterHelp (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/03/ftc-ban-betterhelp-revealing-consumers-data-including-sensitive-mental-health-information-facebook)
- [ ] Rename the events, page titles and URLs you send to analytics and ad tools so that none of them names a condition, a drug or a diagnosis. [If it applies] Sources: California AG on Healthline (https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-announces-largest-ccpa-settlement-date-secures-155), FTC on GoodRx (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/ftc-enforcement-action-bar-goodrx-sharing-consumers-sensitive-health-info-advertising)
- [ ] Never upload emails, phone numbers or ad IDs from a health app to an ad platform for targeting. [If it applies] Sources: FTC on GoodRx (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/ftc-enforcement-action-bar-goodrx-sharing-consumers-sensitive-health-info-advertising), FTC on BetterHelp (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/03/ftc-ban-betterhelp-revealing-consumers-data-including-sensitive-mental-health-information-facebook)
- [ ] Check that your privacy policy names every place health data goes, and that the app does exactly what the policy says. [If it applies] Sources: FTC Flo Health case (https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/192-3133-flo-health-inc), FTC on BetterHelp (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/03/ftc-ban-betterhelp-revealing-consumers-data-including-sensitive-mental-health-information-facebook)
- [ ] Add health data to your breach plan, and treat data sent to a third party without permission as a breach that needs notices. [If it applies] Sources: FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule), Federal Register, 2024 amendments (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-05-30/pdf/2024-10855.pdf)
- [ ] Do not let a third-party SDK or recording tool capture health entries or private messages, because California's recording law needs every party's consent. [If it applies] Sources: Meta 10-Q on the Flo verdict (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326801/000162828026050705/meta-20260630.htm), Penal Code 637.2 (mirror) (https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/penal-code/pen-sect-637-2/)
- [ ] If you have users in Washington and handle anything that could reveal health, have a lawyer check your app against the My Health My Data Act. [If it applies] Sources: RCW 19.373.090 (https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.373.090), Orrick on the first lawsuit (https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2025/02/First-Lawsuit-Filed-Under-Washingtons-My-Health-My-Data-Act)
- [ ] Write down who your app is for, and if it is for a general audience, do not market it to children under 13. [Must do] Sources: FTC COPPA compliance plan (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business), FTC COPPA FAQ (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)
- [ ] When you learn that a user is under 13, for example from a parent's email, get a parent's consent or delete that user's data, and log what you did. [Must do] Sources: FTC COPPA FAQ (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)
- [ ] If you ask for age, use a neutral age gate: empty birth date fields, no default, no "over 12" checkbox, and a cookie so the answer cannot be changed. [If it applies] Sources: FTC COPPA FAQ (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)
- [ ] If your app is directed to children, get verifiable parental consent before you collect personal information, and separate consent before you share it with third parties. [If it applies] Sources: FTC COPPA compliance plan (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business), Federal Register, 2025 COPPA Rule (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-04-22/html/2025-05904.htm)
- [ ] Get verifiable parental consent before analytics or ad SDKs collect persistent identifiers in any part aimed at children, and remove them if you list in the App Store Kids Category. [If it applies] Sources: eCFR 16 CFR 312.2 (https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-10-01/title-16.xml?part=312&section=312.2), Apple guideline 1.3 (Kids Category) (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- [ ] If children use your app, write a security program and a retention policy, and delete children's data when you no longer need it. [If it applies] Sources: Federal Register, 2025 COPPA Rule (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-04-22/html/2025-05904.htm), FTC announcement (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-finalizes-changes-childrens-privacy-rule-limiting-companies-ability-monetize-kids-data)
- [ ] Before you ship a mobile app to users in Texas or Utah, check the current state of their app-store age laws. [If it applies] Sources: InfoLawGroup on Texas (https://www.infolawgroup.com/insights/2026/7/7/supreme-court-clears-the-way-texass-app-store-accountability-act-is-now-enforceable), Loeb and Loeb on Utah (https://www.loeb.com/en/insights/passle/2026/05/update-on-utah-app-store-law--another-waiting-game)
